MHTSdigiXR Email Integration Guide

Hostinger Free Email Setup for mhtsdigixr.com

Document Version: 1.0 | Last Updated: March 2026

PREREQUISITE

You need an active Hostinger hosting plan with the domain mhtsdigixr.com already connected. Hostinger provides 5 free email accounts with most hosting plans. This guide covers creating those email accounts and connecting them to your MHTSdigiXR website for automated emails (password resets, welcome messages, contact form notifications).

Step 1: Create Email Accounts on Hostinger

  1. Log in to your Hostinger hPanel at hpanel.hostinger.com
  2. Click on Emails in the top menu
  3. Select your domain mhtsdigixr.com
  4. Click Create Email Account
  5. Enter the email name (e.g. info) and a strong password
  6. Click Create
  7. Repeat for each email account you want to create (up to 5)

Step 2: Suggested Email Accounts (5 Free)

Here are the recommended email accounts to create for your business:

1
info@mhtsdigixr.com Main business contact. Used for general enquiries, displayed on website footer and contact page.
2
noreply@mhtsdigixr.com Automated system emails. Best choice for SMTP "From Email" in Settings. Used for password resets, welcome emails, and contact form notifications.
3
careers@mhtsdigixr.com Job applications and recruitment. Displayed on the Careers page.
4
support@mhtsdigixr.com Customer support and post-sale communication.
5
admin@mhtsdigixr.com Internal admin communications and account management.

Step 3: Get Your SMTP Credentials

After creating at least one email account, get the SMTP settings:

  1. In hPanel, go to Emails → Email Accounts
  2. Click on your email account (e.g. noreply@mhtsdigixr.com)
  3. Click Connect Devices or Configuration Settings
  4. Note down the Outgoing (SMTP) settings

Hostinger SMTP Settings:

Setting Value
SMTP Host smtp.hostinger.com
SMTP Port 465
Security SSL (Secure = ON)
Username Your full email address (e.g. noreply@mhtsdigixr.com)
Password The password you set when creating the email account
Important: The SMTP username must be the full email address, not just the name part. Use noreply@mhtsdigixr.com, not just noreply.

Step 4: Configure SMTP in Your MHTSdigiXR App

  1. Open your website and go to /accounting/login
  2. Log in as Super Admin:
    • Username: superadmin
    • Password: admin123 (or your custom password)
  3. In the sidebar, click Settings
  4. Scroll down to the SMTP Settings section
  5. Fill in the following fields:
    Field Enter This
    Host smtp.hostinger.com
    Port 465
    Secure (SSL) Toggle ON
    Username noreply@mhtsdigixr.com
    Password Your email account password
    From Name MHTSdigiXR
    From Email noreply@mhtsdigixr.com
  6. Click Save

Step 5: Test Your Email Connection

  1. After saving SMTP settings, click the Test Connection button
  2. A test email will be sent to your logged-in Super Admin email address
  3. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for the test email
  4. If you see "Test email sent successfully", your email integration is complete!
Success! If the test email arrives, all automated emails from your website will now work correctly.

Emails Your App Sends Automatically

Once SMTP is configured, the following emails are sent automatically by the system:

Email Type When It's Sent Sent To
Password Reset When an employee clicks "Forgot Password?" on the login page The employee's email address
Welcome Email When a Super Admin creates a new employee account The new employee's email address
Contact Form Notification When a website visitor submits the Contact Us form The SMTP "From Email" address (admin notification)
SMTP Test When Super Admin clicks "Test Connection" in Settings The Super Admin's email address
Note: If SMTP is not configured, all email features are silently skipped. The website still works normally — contact form messages are saved to the database, employees can still log in, etc. Email is an optional enhancement.

Access Your Email (Webmail)

To read and send emails from your Hostinger accounts:

  1. Go to hpanel.hostinger.com → Emails
  2. Click Webmail next to your email account
  3. Or go directly to: https://mail.hostinger.com
  4. Log in with your full email address and password

Troubleshooting

Problem Solution
"Password reset is not configured" on Forgot Password page SMTP is not set up yet. Follow Steps 3-5 above to configure it.
Test email fails with "Connection refused" Check that Host is smtp.hostinger.com, Port is 465, and Secure is ON.
Test email fails with "Authentication failed" Verify your Username is the full email address and Password is correct. Try logging into webmail first to confirm credentials work.
Emails go to spam folder Make sure your domain has proper DNS records (SPF, DKIM). In hPanel → Emails → DNS Zone, verify these records exist. Hostinger usually sets them up automatically.
Contact form doesn't send notification Contact form messages are always saved to the database regardless of SMTP. The email notification only works when SMTP is configured. Check Contact Inbox in the accounting app.
Password reset link doesn't work Reset links expire after 1 hour. Make sure the employee clicks the link promptly. If expired, they can request a new one.
Port 587 vs 465 Hostinger supports both. Port 465 with SSL (Secure ON) is recommended. Port 587 with STARTTLS also works — set Secure OFF and the app will use STARTTLS automatically.
